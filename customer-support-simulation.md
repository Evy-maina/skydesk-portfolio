# SKYDESK Customer Support Simulation

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how I would handle a customer-service conversation professionally, from understanding the customer's concern to providing a clear and helpful response.

## Scenario

A customer contacts support because their order has not arrived within the expected delivery time.

### Customer Message

> "Hi, I've been waiting for my order and it still hasn't arrived. Can you please help me?"

### My Response

"Hi, thank you for reaching out. I'm sorry your order has been delayed. I understand how frustrating it can be to wait longer than expected.

I'd be happy to help you check the status of your order. Please provide your order number so I can review the available information and guide you on the next steps.

Thank you for your patience."

## My Approach

### 1. Acknowledge

I acknowledge the customer's concern and show empathy.

### 2. Clarify

I request the information needed to investigate the issue.

### 3. Assist

I provide clear next steps instead of leaving the customer uncertain.

### 4. Follow Up

I would continue assisting the customer until the issue is resolved or the appropriate team takes over.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Customer empathy
- Written communication
- Active listening
- Problem solving
- Professional tone
- Clear instructions
- Customer-focused support
- Attention to detail

## Expected Outcome

The customer understands that their concern has been heard and knows what information is needed to move the issue toward resolution.
